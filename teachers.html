Volunteers in Your Classroom

We love our volunteers — and so will you.

From parents and grandparents to community members, volunteers help make events and learning activities successful. The PTA Volunteer Coordinator is here to help you assess your needs, train volunteers, and get people into your classroom.

01

Request volunteers

Email your volunteer needs to the coordinator: what you need help with, when, and how many people.

Email the coordinator →
02

Clearance

All volunteers must be cleared by the district before they can help in classrooms, on field trips, or at events. Not sure someone is cleared? We are happy to check for you.

Ask us to check →
03

Point families our way

If parents in your classroom want to volunteer, send them to the volunteer page to get started — or send them to the coordinator and we'll connect directly.

PTA Meetings

Join us — in person or on Google Meet.

Meetings are where the work gets decided — and teachers' voices carry a lot of weight. Come have a say on what we're working on, or use the time to reach parents directly:

  • Present something you're proud of, a request, or something parents should know.
  • Show off your grade level's projects — how can we present them?

Can't make it in person? Email board@example.org for the Google Meet link.

When we meet

First Wednesday of every month

  • Time 6:00–7:00 PM
  • Where School cafeteria + Google Meet
  • Season September through June
