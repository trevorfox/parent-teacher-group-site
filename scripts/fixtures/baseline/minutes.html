Good to know

Come see how it works.

Minutes go up once they’re approved at the following month’s meeting, so there’s usually about a month’s lag between a meeting and its notes appearing here.

The PTA meets the first Wednesday of every month, September through June, 6:00–7:00 PM, in the cafeteria and on Google Meet. Voting membership is open to every Example Elementary parent and guardian, and there are no dues. You’re welcome to come share an idea or just listen in.
