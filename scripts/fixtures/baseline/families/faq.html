The PTA

Who we are, and how to get involved.

From the Example PTA — independent of the school district

What is the PTA?

The Example PTA is the parent- and staff-run nonprofit behind many of the programs and events at Example Elementary. See what we fund →

It is an independent organization, not administered by the school district. There are no dues, and every parent and guardian is already a member.

When and where are PTA meetings?

The first Wednesday of the month, 6:00–7:00 PM, with a Google Meet option. Everyone is welcome.

PTA & school calendar →

How do I get involved or volunteer?

Come to one meeting, help at one event, or take on a whole project. All of it helps.
