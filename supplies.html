The 5% Back to Schools Program

Don't forget the school ID.

Give this ID every time you pay — to the cashier in any store, or on a phone order at 1-800-GO-DEPOT — and Example Elementary earns credits worth 5% of your school-supply purchase. It works all year long, not just at back-to-school.

Example Elementary's 5% Back to Schools ID

00000000

Basic school and office supplies qualify — paper, pens, binders, crayons, and nearly everything on these lists. Technology (like headphones), ink & toner, and gift cards don't. Credits arrive quarterly as an Office Depot card the school spends on free supplies.

Heads up: the ID has to be given when you pay — orders placed at officedepot.com can't be credited (online checkout has no ID field, and stores can't add it after a purchase). Use the item links to find what you need, then buy in store.
