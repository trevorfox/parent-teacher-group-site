About the PTA

Family & community support for every student.

Our mission

Support learning. Support teaching. Support community.

The Example Elementary PTA is a nonprofit organized to provide family and community support for the students, staff, and administration of Example Elementary Elementary.

We enhance and supplement every student's education through volunteerism and fundraising — paying for enrichment programs and opportunities that reach all students enrolled at the school.

Noncommercial, nonsectarian, and nonpartisan. Voting membership is open to all parents and guardians of Example Elementary students; school staff are honorary members.

Meetings & Membership

Everyone's invited to the table.

The PTA meets once a month from September through June. Come see what we're working on, share an idea, or just listen in. Voting membership is open to all Example Elementary parents and guardians — and there are no dues.
