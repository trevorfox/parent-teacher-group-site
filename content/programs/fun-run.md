`cta` is the button label, and `sponsors` becomes the logo grid. A sponsor with
no logo file in `assets/programs/fun-run/sponsors/` shows as a name tile.

## How the day works

- Every class runs or walks laps for a set time
- Families collect pledges ahead of the event
- Volunteers count laps and hand out water

## Where the money goes

Everything raised funds the programs on the [Programs page](/programs).
