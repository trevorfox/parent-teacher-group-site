Our mission

Support learning. Support teaching. Support community.

The {{org.name}} is a nonprofit organized to provide family and community support for the students, staff, and administration of {{school.nameShort}} Elementary.

We enhance and supplement every student's education through volunteerism and fundraising — paying for enrichment programs and opportunities that reach all students enrolled at the school.

Noncommercial, nonsectarian, and nonpartisan. Voting membership is open to all parents and guardians of {{school.nameShort}} students; school staff are honorary members.
