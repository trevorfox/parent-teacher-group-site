Double your gift

Your employer may match it.

Plenty of large employers match what their people give to schools, which turns a $50 donation into $100 at no extra cost to you. It is usually a short form on your company’s giving portal, and most families never think to check.

How to do it

  1. Donate and keep the emailed receipt.
  2. Open your company’s giving or volunteering portal.
  3. Search for {{org.nameShort}}, or enter our tax ID 00-0000000.
  4. Submit the receipt. The match comes straight to the {{org.abbrev}}.
